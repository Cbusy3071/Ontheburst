On the Burst

Privacy policy

Last updated 1 November 2026

On the Burst ("we", "us") runs ontheburst.com and the On the Burst app. This policy explains what we collect, why, and what you can do about it. We handle personal information in line with the Australian Privacy Principles.

What we collect

  • Account details: your email address, password (stored encrypted by our authentication provider) and date of birth. We use your date of birth only to check you are 16 or older.
  • Profile details you choose to add: name, handle, photo, account type, position, grade, club, region, bio and player card numbers.
  • Content you post: clips, photos, captions, comments, stats, trials and events, likes, saves, follows and reports.
  • Rising Star profiles: for players under 16, the parent or guardian who creates the profile gives us the player's name, position, grade, club and date of birth, and controls everything posted on it.
  • Usage needed to run the feed: which clips you view and watch to the end, so we can count views and rank highlights.
  • Technical data: our hosting providers log IP addresses and browser details to keep the service secure and working.

What we don't do

  • We don't sell your information or share it with advertisers.
  • We don't run ads, third-party analytics, tracking pixels or session recording.
  • We don't load fonts or scripts from third-party servers when you use the site.
  • There is no private messaging.

Who can see what

Profiles and posts are public to other signed-in users and are designed to be seen by clubs, scouts and coaches. Your email address and date of birth are never shown to other users. Saved posts are visible only to you.

Where it's stored

Your data is stored with Supabase (database, authentication and file storage) and the site is served by GitHub Pages behind Cloudflare. These providers may store or process data outside Australia, including in the United States. We use them only to run the service.

Cookies and local storage

We store your sign-in session and your light/dark mode choice in your browser. We don't use advertising or tracking cookies.

Emails

We send account emails only, such as sign-up confirmation and password resets. We don't send marketing emails. If we ever do, they will need your consent and will include an unsubscribe link and our contact details.

Your choices

  • Edit or delete anything you've posted at any time.
  • Delete your account in Settings. This deletes your profile, posts, clips, comments and likes, and any Rising Star profiles you manage.
  • Ask us for a copy of your information, or to correct it, by emailing support@ontheburst.com.

Children

You must be 16 or older to have an account. Players under 16 can only appear as Rising Star profiles created and run by a parent or guardian, who approves every comment. If you think someone under 16 has an account, report it in the app or email us and we will remove it.

Complaints

Email support@ontheburst.com and we'll respond within 30 days. If you're not satisfied, you can contact the Office of the Australian Information Commissioner at oaic.gov.au.

Changes

If we change this policy in a way that matters, we'll tell you in the app before it takes effect.
